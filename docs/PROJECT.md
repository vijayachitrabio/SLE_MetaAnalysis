# Project: Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE) European Meta-analysis

## 🎯 Executive Summary
This project represents a large-scale meta-analysis of SLE in individuals of European ancestry. By integrating two major discovery cohorts (Bentham 2015 and FinnGen R12) and validating findings in an independent Spanish cohort, we have identified **47 independent risk loci**, including **22 putatively novel associations**.

## 🧬 Scientific Background
Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE) is a chronic autoimmune disease characterized by multisystem involvement and the production of autoantibodies. While many risk loci have been identified, significant missing heritability remains. This study leverages increased sample sizes and modern causal inference methods (LAVA/COLOC) to prioritize high-confidence therapeutic targets.

## 🏆 Key Findings
- **47 Independent Loci**: Final genome-wide significant count after clumping and Spanish validation.
- **22 Novel Loci**: Signals not previously reported in the GWAS Catalog or major SLE publications.
- **CLIC1 Causal Driver**: High-confidence colocalization (PP4=0.94) identifying *CLIC1* as a major driver in Whole Blood.
- **Pathway Convergence**: Strong enrichment in Type I Interferon, JAK-STAT signaling, and B-cell activation pathways.

## 🤝 Target Audiences

- **Translational Scientists**: Focused on the 15 prioritized "High-Confidence" therapeutic targets.
- **Clinicians**: Seeking to understand the genetic basis of autoimmune pleiotropy.
